Holistic, non-clinical support for adolescents, educators & communities

Early Emotional Support

Non‑diagnostic listening circles, psychoeducation and guided referrals to clinical partners when needed.

  • Coping skills development
  • Psychoeducation & emotional regulation

Workshops & Training

Mental health awareness, resilience facilitation for educators and youth workers.

  • Mental health awareness sessions
  • Stress management workshops
  • Trauma-informed facilitation training

Capacity Building

Empowering organisations to embed mental health awareness into their programmes.

  • Identifying emotional distress
  • Referral pathway guidance
  • Programme adaptation support

Monitoring & Evaluation

  • Attendance tracking
  • Pre- & post-reflection tools
  • Summary reports for partners

Adaptation & Consult

  • Ethical safeguarding frameworks

⚜️ Our Ethical Mandate

Mi Future Leaders provides mental health education and early support services only.
We do not offer psychotherapy, psychological diagnosis, or clinical treatment.
