About Us

Structured, trauma-informed mental health education — prevention, not clinical intervention.

⚜️ Our Mission

To create a safe space for future leaders to express their mental health-related issues and apply possible intervention strategies to improve their mental health status, with priority given to disadvantaged communities and children/youth from adverse backgrounds.

⚜️ Our Vision

  • Build leaders with a clear mindset
  • Eradicate the stigma around mental health
  • Provide quality counselling services and safe spaces for children

Who We Are

Mi Future Leaders provides structured, trauma-informed mental health education programmes for adolescents in under-resourced communities. Our services focus on prevention, emotional skills development, and early support rather than clinical intervention or therapy.

Resilient Minds Programme

6-week group-based emotional literacy programme delivered in public schools and community centres. Topics include: understanding emotions, stress management, healthy coping, boundaries, identity, and help-seeking.

Counselling Services

Non-clinical, trauma-informed emotional support. Short-term supportive counselling focusing on coping skills, emotional regulation, and psychoeducation. Referrals provided for clinical needs.

Workshop & Facilitation

Mental health awareness sessions, stress management workshops, and resilience facilitation for youth, educators, and community workers. Custom half-day or full-day formats available.

M&E & Reporting

Comprehensive monitoring and evaluation: attendance tracking, pre/post reflection tools, facilitator notes, and impact reports for partners and funders.

Referral & Signposting

Warm connections to trusted clinical partners including psychologists, social workers, and community health resources. Ethical boundary maintained — we do not provide therapy.

Capacity Building

Training for educators and youth workers: identifying emotional distress, referral pathway guidance, programme adaptation support, and trauma-informed practice embedding.
