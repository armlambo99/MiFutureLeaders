⚜️ Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

What services does Mi Future Leaders provide?

We provide trauma-informed mental health education, 6-week Resilient Minds programmes, non-clinical emotional support, workshops, and referral pathways to clinical partners. We do not provide psychotherapy or clinical diagnosis.

How can my school partner with you?

Reach out via our contact form or email partnerships@mifutureleaders.org. We'll schedule a discovery call to understand your school's needs and tailor our programmes accordingly.

Do you offer counselling services?

We offer non-clinical emotional support and psychoeducation. For clinical therapy needs, we provide warm referrals to trusted clinical partners including psychologists and social workers.

How can I donate to support your work?

Click the DONATE button in the navigation bar. You can make a secure online donation via credit/debit card. Your contribution helps us reach more young people in underserved communities.

Do you work outside Cape Town and Johannesburg?

Yes! We work with partners across South Africa. Our programmes can be delivered virtually or through trained facilitators in various regions.
